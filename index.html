What we do

Compliance support for every step of the shipment

From the first question about a product to the paperwork on the dock, we help keep dangerous goods shipments organized and moving.

DG classification

Classification across all 9 hazard classes, including SDS review, UN number identification, packing group and proper shipping name.

Documentation & DGD

Dangerous Goods Declarations, shipper's declarations and the paperwork for air, ocean and ground.

Packaging & labeling

UN-certified packaging sourcing, plus compliant marking, labeling and placarding to IATA, IMDG and 49 CFR.

DG inspections

Pre-shipment inspections, with repackaging and relabeling to fix issues before they delay your freight.

DG emergency support

Last-minute shipment or a compliance problem that needs fixing today? Call us for urgent help.

DG consulting

Ongoing compliance guidance, regulatory updates and support for complex or restricted commodities.

Temperature control

Help keeping temperature-sensitive shipments in range, from insulated packaging and coolants to planning around transit time.

Dry ice services

Packaging, marking, labeling and paperwork for shipments packed with dry ice, by air or ground.
