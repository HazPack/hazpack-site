Privacy

What happens to the details you send us through this website.

What we collect

When you send the quote or contact form, we receive what you type in: your name, email, and anything else you choose to add, such as company, phone number and details about your shipment.

This website does not use advertising or analytics trackers, and the public pages do not set cookies.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you, prepare your quote and carry out work you ask us to do. We do not sell your details or share them for marketing.

Services that help run the site

  • Web3Forms delivers form submissions to our email inbox.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

Client Portal

If you have a Client Portal login, we keep the company, job and contact details needed to run your account, and the portal uses a cookie to keep you signed in.
